# Standard Engineering Programme Template Catalogue

The platform provides 15 pre-configured engineering programme templates.

## Catalogue

### 1. B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (`BE-CSE`)
- **Degree Type**: Bachelor of Engineering
- **Duration**: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Suggested Department**: Computer Science and Engineering (`CSE`)
- **Description**: Comprehensive degree programme focusing on computer systems, software engineering, algorithms, and computing hardware.
